Case summary

Present assortment and characteristics without a heavy interface and connect the request to internal accounting. Selected items and contact data form a ready-to-process inquiry while an integration layer connects the site to the internal system. The buyer does not re-enter the same data manually and the manager receives a request in a usable structure.
