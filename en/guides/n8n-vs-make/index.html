n8n vs Make choose by the critical workflow.

The useful choice is not about which platform is more popular. It depends on where data lives, how custom the logic is, whether self-hosting matters and who will maintain the workflow after launch.

Short answer

Choose n8n when custom APIs, code, self-hosting and deeper workflow control matter. Make is often faster for straightforward SaaS integrations when managed infrastructure and ready-made modules are more valuable than low-level flexibility.

01 / Guide

When n8n is a strong fit

01

Own infrastructure

The workflow can run on your VPS or controlled environment.

02

Custom APIs

HTTP calls, transformations and custom code are part of the normal flow.

03

Operational control

Logs, secrets and execution behavior need more direct control.

04

Product-like workflows

Automation is gradually becoming part of an internal software product.

02 / Guide

When Make is practical

01

Fast SaaS integration

Popular services are covered by ready-made modules.

02

Managed hosting

You do not want to maintain a server for the automation layer.

03

Straightforward process

The workflow is relatively linear and module-friendly.

04

Non-developer ownership

Operations or marketing staff may need to understand the scenario.

03 / Guide

What matters more than the platform

01

Source of truth

Business state should live in CRM, a database or another primary system.

02

Retry behavior

Repeated events and failed steps must be safe to rerun.

03

Alerting

Someone needs to know when a critical workflow fails.

04

Secrets and access

Credentials and permissions need a deliberate operational model.
